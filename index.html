Support

How do I earn?

Watch rewarded ads to the end. Each completed ad adds TON to your balance, up to 20 ads a day.

Why am I asked a quick question?

It confirms you are a real person. You solve it once and it lasts for your session.

When do I get paid?

Withdrawals join a payout queue and are sent to your connected wallet as ad revenue arrives. The minimum is 0.5 TON.

An ad did not pay. Why?

Ads must be watched to the end. Nothing is credited after the daily limit or if the quick check was skipped.

Terms and privacy

Rewards are paid in TON and funded by ad revenue. Your balance only grows from ads you watch to the end.

Withdrawals are queued and sent to the wallet you connect. The minimum withdrawal is 0.5 TON.

Vireo is not an investment, a bank, or financial advice. Crypto prices change and rewards are not guaranteed.

Bots, multiple accounts and fake views are not allowed, and rewards may be withheld for abuse.

We store your Telegram ID, your balance, your ad history and the wallet address you withdraw to. We do not sell your data.
